## 🎮 About the Game

WebCam: Behind The Screen is a 3D adult life simulation game developed and published by Cream Corp. It was released for Windows on August 27, 2026. The game follows an aspiring webcam model who streams, fulfills subscriber requests, earns tips, manages her appearance, and balances food, exercise, rest, and daily activities.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **WebCam: Behind The Screen**. This trainer enhances the single-player management loop by giving players control over money, energy, physical condition, popularity, and wardrobe progression. It is designed for experimenting with outfits, streaming schedules, lifestyle choices, and subscriber activities without the normal resource limitations.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore the game's streaming, wardrobe, lifestyle, popularity, and body-management systems without repeating the normal income and resource grind.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

The game is primarily experienced through the character's apartment and activity spaces. Move between available interaction points, computers, furniture, food, exercise equipment, and personal-care locations. The trainer does not add movement abilities; it supports the resources required to perform activities freely.

### Tools & Inventory

Use the inventory and wardrobe systems to manage clothing, food, and usable lifestyle items. Infinite Money makes purchases easier, while Unlock Wardrobe provides access to available clothing options for streaming, selfies, and other photo-based activities.

### World & Environment

The main gameplay loop centers on managing the character's private life and online career. Stream for viewers, respond to subscriber requests, take photos and selfies, receive deliveries, maintain personal care, exercise, eat, and spend earnings on clothing and lifestyle improvements.

### Combat & Weapons

WebCam: Behind The Screen has no conventional combat system. The trainer focuses on simulation resources, progression, wardrobe access, popularity, and daily-life management instead of combat enhancements.
